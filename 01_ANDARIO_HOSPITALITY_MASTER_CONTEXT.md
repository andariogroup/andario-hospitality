## 3. Positioning

Andario Hospitality is a hybrid business combining consulting, technology and digital services, focused specifically on accommodation and tourism establishments.

The initial market is Colombia, with a strategic objective of expanding progressively into Latin America.

The brand seeks to become the digital partner of small and medium-sized independent accommodations, helping them take their businesses to a new level through simple, accessible and connected digital solutions.

## 4. Target customer

Primary audience:

- Small and medium-sized independent accommodations
- Hostels
- Small hotels
- Inns/posadas
- Tourist apartments
- Apart-hotels
- Cabins
- Villas
- Rural accommodations
- Entrepreneurs renting accommodation by the day

The commercial positioning should not depend on a rigid unit-count limit. The key characteristic is that the business is independent and needs practical digitalization without the complexity of a large hospitality platform.

## 5. Main problems

The principal problems Andario aims to solve are:

1. They do not have an integrated digital strategy.
2. They depend excessively on OTAs.
3. They do not have their own website.
4. They do not have a reservation engine.
5. They do not have an SEO strategy.
6. They do not have analytics.

Other related problems include fragmented information, disconnected channels, dependence on WhatsApp for manual processes, weak digital presentation, lack of measurement, and lack of a structured direct-booking channel.

## 6. Core transformation

Andario helps an accommodation move from fragmented digital presence to an integrated digital strategy and its own digital growth channel.

Before:
Google + Instagram + Facebook + WhatsApp + OTAs + disconnected tools.

Target state:
Strategy + website + visibility + direct booking + communication + content + analytics, connected around the establishment.

## 7. Value proposition

### Primary proposition

**Digitalizamos pequeños alojamientos para convertir su presencia en Internet en una herramienta real de crecimiento.**

### Supporting proposition

**Estrategia, tecnología y acompañamiento. En un solo lugar.**

### Brand idea

**Andario Hospitality is the digital partner for independent accommodations.**

The brand does not promise guaranteed increases in bookings, rankings or revenue. It provides the infrastructure, strategy and ongoing improvement framework needed to build a stronger digital presence and direct-booking capability.

## 8. Service portfolio

### 01 — Digital Check
Digital diagnosis and strategy.

Purpose:
Understand the establishment's current digital situation, identify gaps and opportunities, prioritize actions and create a personalized roadmap.

### 02 — Andario Web
Professional website.

Purpose:
Create the accommodation's own digital home, designed for trust, information, visibility and conversion.

### 03 — Andario Visibility
SEO, Google and digital visibility.

Purpose:
Improve the establishment's ability to be discovered through relevant organic and local searches.

### 04 — Andario Booking Engine
Direct booking technology.

Purpose:
Provide a proprietary reservation channel and centralize availability, rates, reservations and related booking logic.

### 05 — Andario Connect
WhatsApp, communication and automation.

Purpose:
Connect customer communication with the commercial and reservation journey and progressively automate repetitive interactions.

### 06 — Andario Content
Photography and content.

Purpose:
Present the accommodation, its spaces, services, experiences and destination professionally.

### 07 — Andario Growth
Analytics and optimization.

Purpose:
Measure traffic, interactions, conversion events and reservation activity so decisions can be based on data and the digital ecosystem can evolve.

## 9. Commercial model

Services are sold individually.

A commercial proposal is assembled according to the customer's needs rather than forcing every establishment into one fixed package.

Reference implementation range:
COP $2–4 million approximately.

Reference recurring range:
COP $100,000–200,000 per month approximately.

Exact prices are not to be published as fixed prices on the initial website. The primary commercial CTA is a digital diagnosis / consultation, followed by a personalized proposal.

Commercial relationship model:
**Implementation + recurring service + optional additional services.**
