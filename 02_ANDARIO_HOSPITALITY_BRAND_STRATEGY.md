# ANDARIO HOSPITALITY — BRAND STRATEGY
Version: 1.0

## Brand

**Andario Hospitality**

A commercial line of Andario Group specialized in digitalization for small and medium-sized independent accommodations.

## Brand essence

**El partner digital de tu alojamiento.**

## Positioning statement

Andario Hospitality combines consulting, technology and digital services to help small and medium-sized independent accommodations professionalize their digital presence, build direct channels and progressively improve their digital operation.

## Target

Independent accommodation businesses in Colombia initially, with future expansion into Latin America.

## Core customer problem

The establishment may be present across Google, OTAs, Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp, but its digital ecosystem is often fragmented and lacks a coherent strategy.

## Transformation

From:

- fragmented presence
- dependence on OTAs
- no website
- no booking engine
- no SEO strategy
- no analytics

To:

- integrated digital strategy
- own website
- digital visibility
- direct booking capability
- connected communication
- content
- analytics and continuous improvement

## Value proposition

**Digitalizamos pequeños alojamientos para convertir su presencia en Internet en una herramienta real de crecimiento.**

Secondary message:

**Estrategia, tecnología y acompañamiento. En un solo lugar.**

## Brand personality

The brand should feel:

- Modern
- Technological
- Close
- Innovative
- Reliable
- Simple
- Accessible
- Entrepreneurial

## Tone of voice

Use:

- Clear language
- Professional but human communication
- Practical explanations
- Evidence-based claims
- Hospitality vocabulary
- Simple technical explanations
- Confident but not arrogant language

Avoid:

- Excessive jargon
- Aggressive sales language
- Guaranteed results
- Artificial urgency
- Empty superlatives
- "We are the best" claims
- Complex enterprise terminology when speaking to small businesses

## Differentiators

1. Specialization
2. Simplicity
3. Proprietary technology
4. Close accompaniment

## What we are not

- Traditional marketing agency
- Giant PMS
- Promise-based growth seller
- Price-only competitor

## Strategic narrative

The customer does not need more disconnected tools.

The customer needs a digital strategy that connects:

**presence → visibility → trust → communication → reservation → measurement → improvement.**

## Brand architecture

Andario Group
→ Andario Hospitality
→ Seven services
→ Andario Booking Engine as proprietary technology

## Vision

Ser el partner digital de referencia para pequeños y medianos alojamientos independientes en Colombia y, progresivamente, en Latinoamérica.

## Mission

Ayudar a pequeños y medianos establecimientos de alojamiento a digitalizar y profesionalizar sus negocios mediante soluciones sencillas, accesibles y conectadas.

## Suggested headline territory

- Digitalización para pequeños alojamientos.
- Tu alojamiento ya existe. Ahora hagámoslo crecer en Internet.
- De una presencia digital fragmentada a una estrategia digital integrada.
- Tecnología para llevar tu alojamiento a otro nivel.

The final website headline will be selected during the Website Blueprint and Content Specification stages.
